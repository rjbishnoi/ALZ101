Performance overview · Sensitivity × Specificity

A · Amyloid

Aβ pathology

Markers: Aβ42/40 ratio (CSF or plasma); amyloid PET (florbetapir, florbetaben, flutemetamol).

Earliest detectable: ~20 years before symptoms.

Required for: Anti-amyloid antibody eligibility (lecanemab, donanemab).

CSF Aβ42/40 < 0.05

Amyloid PET SUVR > 1.10

Plasma Aβ42/40 < 0.10

T · Tau

Tau pathology

Markers: Plasma p-tau217 (most sensitive); CSF p-tau181/217; tau PET (flortaucipir).

Stage tracking: Tau PET tracks Braak staging spatially.

Plasma p-tau217: 94% sensitivity for AD pathology (Mayo Clinic data).

p-tau217 elevated

CSF p-tau181 > 60 pg/mL

Tau PET Braak III–IV+

N · Neurodegeneration

+ I = Inflammation

Markers: Volumetric MRI (hippocampal atrophy); FDG-PET hypometabolism; CSF or plasma NfL.

Inflammation (I): GFAP added in 2024 NIA-AA framework as inflammation marker.

Non-specific: Reflects late-stage damage; not AD-specific.

Hippocampal volume <10th %ile

FDG-PET temporoparietal hypometabolism

Plasma NfL elevated

Stepwise Diagnostic Workup

Brief screen → Extended assessment → Pattern-based subspecialty workup

A modern memory-clinic workup follows three stages. The brief screen flags whether further evaluation is needed. The extended assessment characterizes what is wrong. Once a clinical pattern is recognized, targeted biomarker and imaging tests are chosen by syndrome.
