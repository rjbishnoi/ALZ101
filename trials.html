For patients & families

Want to participate in research?

Talk to your neurologist or memory clinic. Most academic centers have a research coordinator.

National registries:

  • TrialMatch — Alzheimer's Association
  • Brain Health Registry — UCSF
  • APT Webstudy — USC
  • GeneMatch — for APOE-based trials

Most trials reimburse travel. Many are virtual / decentralized.

Therapeutic landscape (2026)

Anti-amyloid — first generation (lecanemab, donanemab) approved; next-gen (trontinemab, remternetug) entering pivotal readouts.

Anti-tau — antibodies (E2814, JNJ-2056) and active vaccines (AADvac1) in Ph 2/3.

Metabolic — GLP-1 agonists (semaglutide EVOKE) testing dementia repurposing hypothesis.

DLB-specific — neflamapimod (p38α) showing functional benefits.

Eligibility commonalities

Most early-AD trials require:

  • Aβ confirmation (PET or CSF p-tau217)
  • MMSE 22–28 or CDR 0.5–1
  • Stable medications ≥4 wk
  • MRI without significant micro-bleeds
  • Reliable study partner
