14 modifiable risk factors — Lancet Commission 2024

PAR = Population-attributable risk

The Lancet Commission identifies 14 modifiable factors that, if eliminated, could prevent or delay up to 45% of dementia cases globally. Bars show population-attributable risk by life stage.

CLINICAL TAKEAWAY

Mid-life (45–65 years) is the highest-leverage window. Hearing loss, hypertension, and elevated LDL together account for ~16% of population-attributable risk — all directly modifiable.
