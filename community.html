GUIDE Model (CMS 2024)

CMS GUIDE program offers comprehensive dementia care including 24/7 access line, care navigator, respite benefit ($2,500/yr).

Available through participating practices through 2032. See guide.cms.gov for participating sites.

PACE programs

All-inclusive comprehensive elderly care for dual-eligible (Medicare + Medicaid). Combines day program, medical, social, and home care.

Available in 32 states + DC at 175+ sites. Find at npaonline.org.

Memory care vs. assisted living

Assisted living — for moderate ADL needs, generally not secured.

Memory care — secured unit, dementia-trained staff, structured activities, locked exits, higher staff ratios. Typically $1,500–3,000/mo more.

Touring a facility — what to look for

VISIT

Visit unannounced

Drop in at meal-time. Are residents engaged? Do staff greet residents by name? Is the unit calm or chaotic?

STAFF

Ask about staff ratios

Day shift 1:6 or better; night 1:10 or better is ideal. Ask about turnover (high turnover = warning sign).

TRAINING

Dementia-specific training

What programs (Teepa Snow's GEMS, Alzheimer's Association essentiALZ)? How many hours required for new hires?

ACTIVITIES

Engagement program

Look for tailored activities, music, intergenerational visits, outdoor access. Avoid TV-as-default.

M E D I C A T I O N

Antipsychotic rates

Ask: what % of residents are on antipsychotics? National benchmark ~14%; lower is generally better for dementia care quality.

D I S C H A R G E

Discharge policies

Under what conditions would they ask the resident to leave? Watch for places that quickly transfer when behaviors escalate.

Spend-down for Medicaid

Medicaid eligibility requires meeting income and asset limits (vary by state; typically ~$2,000 in countable assets for individuals).

Many families spend savings on care until eligible. Consult an elder-law attorney for legal asset protection — there is a 5-year look-back on transfers.

VA Aid & Attendance

Wartime veterans (or surviving spouses) needing help with ADLs may qualify for up to $2,727/mo (single vet, 2025) tax-free.

Apply through VA-accredited claims agent or VSO. Free help; avoid paid-for-application services.

Tax considerations

Medical expense deduction: amounts above 7.5% of AGI may be deductible. Includes nursing home, assisted living (if primarily medical), in-home care.

Dependent care credit if claiming the relative as a dependent.
